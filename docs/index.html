A green tick that was someone else's wildcard

My first sending domain was parked on Afternic, whose zone answers every TXT question with the same line. The email tool asked for _dmarc, the wildcard replied, and the dashboard showed Verified. Nothing had been configured.

$ nslookup -type=TXT mionegozio.com
mionegozio.com          text = "v=spf1 -all"
$ nslookup -type=TXT _dmarc.mionegozio.com
_dmarc.mionegozio.com   text = "v=spf1 -all"      # identical

Since then I read the records in public DNS, never on a dashboard tick. This workflow is that habit, automated.

What it cannot see

  • Whether a message lands in the inbox.DNS says who may send. Reputation, content and complaints decide the rest.
  • DKIM keys without a selector.A key is found only by its selector name, which you read from a real email.
  • SPF on the return-path.Email tools often send with their own hidden bounce address. The check reads the domain you type.
  • Domains you did not list.A tool sending from a subdomain nobody told you about stays invisible until DMARC reports show it.
