Four things that broke for real

Found while authenticating send.negozio-online.org and running the flows. Logs and screenshots are from the field notes.

Smart Sending ate the first cart email

A welcome email had gone out under five hours earlier, so Klaviyo skipped email 2.1. No error, no alert: one screen says why.

17:47  Received Email   "Benvenuto in Torrefazione Nord"
18:45  Checkout Started
22:45  2.1 due  ->  Delivered 0, Skipped 1
       "Skipped: Smart Sending (1)"

Rule: Smart Sending off on abandoned cart, on for newsletters.

Double opt-in did not protect the list

A sign-up through Shopify's newsletter field landed as subscribed 86 milliseconds after the webhook. No confirmation email was ever sent.

consent            SUBSCRIBED
method             SHOPIFY / Customer Webhook
webhook event      15:30:36.569
consent_timestamp  15:30:36.655

Rule: keep consent source and timestamp on every profile (GDPR art. 7(1)) and audit for single opt-in leaks.

A green "Verified" was someone else's wildcard

The first domain was parked on Afternic, whose zone answers every TXT query. Klaviyo asked for _dmarc, the wildcard replied and the check went green.

$ nslookup -type=TXT mionegozio.com
mionegozio.com          text = "v=spf1 -all"
$ nslookup -type=TXT _dmarc.mionegozio.com
_dmarc.mionegozio.com   text = "v=spf1 -all"

Rule: verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC in public DNS, never on a dashboard tick.

Good copy promised what the store cannot do

Drafts passed a manual read while promising shipping times and a coupon expiry the store does not offer.

the draft said"spedizione garantita in 48 ore"
store policy sayspublished_shipping_sla: null
the draft said"valido solo 14 giorni"
store policy sayssupports_per_recipient_expiry: false

Rule: every draft goes through the pre-send gate against config/store-facts-and-promo-policy.json.

The gate itself

  1. Personalisation fallbacksKlaviyo Liquid and HubSpot HubL tags with a missing or empty fallback.
  2. GA4 UTMsLowercase utm_source, utm_medium=email and utm_campaign on every commercial link.
  3. Copy against store policyNo shipping times the checkout does not publish, no expiry on a static code.
  4. Discount exclusionsVIPs, recent buyers and active CX holds excluded whenever a discount appears.
  5. Mobile and deliverability basicsSubject up to 50 characters, a preview text that adds something, the unsubscribe tag, image alt text.
