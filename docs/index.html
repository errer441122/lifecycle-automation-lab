4 Real Platform Failure Modes Caught Hands-On (Klaviyo · Shopify · Cloudflare)

None of these are textbook theory — every failure below occurred while authenticating a live sending subdomain (send.negozio-online.org) and firing real Shopify-to-Klaviyo lifecycle flows (read full field notes).

Silent Revenue Loss

1. Smart Sending Silently Ate Cart Email #1

A Welcome email went out 5 hours earlier, so Klaviyo skipped the high-intent Abandoned Cart touch with zero visible errors in standard campaign reports.

Operational Rule: Turn Smart Sending OFF on Abandoned Cart & Checkout recovery; keep ON only for promotional newsletters.
GDPR Art. 7(1) Risk

2. Shopify Checkout Checkbox Bypassed Double Opt-In

Enabling Double Opt-In on a Klaviyo list only protects Klaviyo forms — Shopify's native checkout checkbox subscribed contacts in 86ms with zero confirmation email.

Operational Rule: Isolate flow lists, block single-opt-in leaks, and log explicit consent_source + timestamp for every profile.
Deliverability Trap

3. A Green "Verified" DNS Badge Was a Wildcard

A parked domain answered _dmarc with a wildcard v=spf1 -all and turned Klaviyo's UI green. Even after fixing DNS, a Verified domain still requires manual Activation.

Operational Rule: Never trust UI green checkmarks alone — verify SPF, DKIM, and DMARC in public DNS & raw email headers.
Copy vs. Policy Mismatch

4. Email Copy Promised What the Store Could Not Deliver

Manual proofreading missed "48h guaranteed shipping" (unpublished SLA) and "valid 14 days" written on a static coupon code, plus a blank Liquid greeting.

Operational Rule: Run every draft through the automated n8n Pre-Flight QA gate against store-facts-and-promo-policy.json.
