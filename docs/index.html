1. Four Real Platform Failure Modes Caught Hands-On

Discovered by actually authenticating a sending domain (send.negozio-online.org) and firing live Klaviyo flows connected to Shopify (full write-up in docs/field-notes/).

Smart Sending Silently Ate Cart Email 2.1

A welcome email went out 5 hours earlier, so Klaviyo skipped the first abandoned-cart touch with zero errors in standard reports. Rule: turn Smart Sending OFF on high-intent cart touches; keep ON for newsletters.

Shopify Checkbox Bypassed Double Opt-In

Setting Double Opt-In on a Klaviyo list only protects Klaviyo forms — Shopify's native checkbox subscribed contacts in 86ms without confirmation. Rule: isolate flow lists and audit consent_source + timestamp (GDPR Art. 7(1)).

A Green "Verified" DNS Badge Was a Wildcard

A parked domain answered _dmarc with v=spf1 -all and turned Klaviyo green. Plus a Verified domain still needed manual Activation. Rule: always verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC via public DNS and raw headers.

Copy Promised What the Store Could Not Deliver

Human proofreading missed "48h shipping" (unpublished SLA) and "valid 14 days" on a static coupon code, plus empty Liquid fallbacks. Fix: automated AI Copy-vs-Store-Policy QA before scheduling.
